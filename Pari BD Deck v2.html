Problem 02

DeFi "Credit": Three Structural Flaws

Not Real Credit
  • DeFi borrowing is overcollateralized — you borrow less than your posted assets
  • Loans are non-recourse — default recovery relies entirely on collateral liquidation
  • Real credit is undercollateralized — creditworthiness determines cost of borrowing
Not Tradable
  • No market to trade on a single borrower's creditworthiness
  • DeFi lending is borrower-specific — A's debt on AAVE cannot be transferred to B
  • RWA fixed-income assets designed for buy-and-hold — no active trading or hedging
Not Market-Determined
  • DeFi lending yield set by pool utilization ratios & risk models — not actual market participants
  • Credit scores evaluate creditworthiness via backward-looking snapshots
  • In TradFi, market-implied pricing always leads rating agencies — e.g. the 2008 crisis

Problem 03

Disconnected Permissioned / Permissionless Markets

Permissioned (TradFi)
  • Regulatory barriers — RWA fixed-income largely requires KYC; tokenized assets not readily tradable
  • One-way street — tokenized assets mirror underlying prices; price discovery stays off-chain
  • Blended risk baskets — investors forced to take duration + credit + liquidity + counterparty risk together
  • TradFi hesitates — crypto fixed income lacks sustainability and standardization
Permissionless (DeFi)
  • Refused access — permissionless markets cannot access regulated RWA instruments
  • No standardization — crypto borrowings are bilateral in non-standard terms (seniority, default triggers)
  • Pseudo-credit unpriced — custodied CEX assets and accrued staking returns are implicit, untraded credit exposure
  • Structural gap — regulatory barriers not being resolved via market structure redesign
Neither side benefits from the other's liquidity or infrastructure.

Solution

Trade, Hedge & Earn from Credit Risk

Trade
Bet on the default probability of any institution to rise or fall. Price is set entirely by you — not a model, not an oracle feed.
Example: Microstrategy's default probability rises 30% → 45%. Upbet holder marks to market and profits.
Hedge
Protect exposure to an institution's default — custodied assets, loaned capital, prime brokerage balances — without leaving the chain.
Example: Crypto MM holds $50M on Bybit. Upbets as on-chain insurance against a Bybit default event.
Earn
Collect real yield by taking on credit risk as a Downbetter. Premium income comes from credit risk — not from token emissions.
Example: DeFi yield vault Downbets on Tesla credit, earning 7% sustainable yield without token farming.

"Your trades — not models, utilization ratios, or oracle feeds — determine the yield. Mr. Market runs the show."

Mechanics

How Credit Betting Works

Example Market
Microstrategy Default · Expiry Jun 2026
8%
Mark: 8%  ·  1-year hazard rate  ·  Downbet earns ~8% pa
UPBET
Hazard rate rises → mark-to-market gain. Pay running premium at the mark. Receive full notional on a credit event.
DOWNBET
Hazard rate falls → mark-to-market gain. Earn running premium at the mark (~8% pa). Lose full notional on a credit event.
Key Differences vs. Prediction Markets
1
Periodic premium cash flows
Upbet pays Downbet every epoch if no default — creating a continuous yield stream, not a binary outcome.
2
Zero-recovery design
On a credit event, Upbet collects full notional; Downbet loses full notional. No partial-recovery haircut — payout is binary and complete.
3
ISDA-standard default events
Determinations Committee makes a single, final ruling — no arbitration, no ambiguity. Non-ISDA-covered entities settle via a USDC-bonded optimistic mechanism rather than a DC ruling.
4
Path-dependent mark-to-market
Unrealized gains realized without default ever occurring — just like CDS in TradFi (e.g. MtGox, FTX).

Token Design

Token & Governance: Skin in the Game

Without Token Stake
  • —Trade without leverage — Upbetters pay full premium upfront; Downbetters post full notional
  • —No default determination power
  • —No revenue share
  • —No governance vote
With Token Stake
  • ✓Leveraged trading — trade with initial & variation margin rules (IM/VM)
  • ✓Default determination — only stakers can motion credit events to the Determinations Committee
  • ✓Revenue share — portion of trading fees, margin management revenue, default fund income
  • ✓Governance — elect DC; vote on trading rules, IM/VM ratios, default fund contributions, new market listings
Default Waterfall — Accountability Mechanism
IM → Defaulter's Default Fund → Staked Tokens → Project DF → Assessment → Unrealized Profit Haircutting. Stakers bear loss — aligning their incentives with long-term ecosystem prudence.
